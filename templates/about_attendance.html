Version {{ app_version }} Released {{ released }} · Installed for {{ barangay_name }}

An offline queue, service and attendance system for barangay offices. One Windows desktop app, with no internet and no server to maintain.

{{ icon('check', 22) }} What's New in Version {{ app_version }}

  • A new look, with a light and a dark mode (the sun and moon button at the top).
  • Any barangay can use it: the barangay name, logos and signatories are typed in at first start and can be changed any time in Settings.
  • The printed attendance report has the barangay's own header and logos, and signature lines for the Secretary (Prepared by) and the Captain (Noted by).
  • The app logs out by itself after 10 minutes without activity, so records are not left open at the desk.
  • A backup is made automatically every day, without slowing down the front desk.
  • Registration asks for the visitor's age and address, and they are kept in the attendance records, the printed report and the download. They are never printed on the queue slip or shown on the display.
  • If a visitor aged 60 or older is put in the Regular lane, a reminder suggests the Priority lane.

{{ icon('file-text', 22) }} Quick Help

The queue slip does not print

Check that the printer is on, has paper, and its cable is plugged in. Then open Maintenance, choose the printer under Receipt printer, and press Print test slip.

I forgot my password

On the login page, click Forgot password? and type the recovery key (it starts with BQS-) that was saved during first-time setup. If the recovery key is lost too, contact the developers.

The names on the printed report are wrong or missing

Open Settings and edit the Signatories. The next report you print uses the new names.

We need old records back, or the computer broke

The app makes a backup every day and keeps one for each of the last 14 days it was used. You can also save a copy to a USB drive on the Maintenance page. Do not copy or replace the app's files yourself: contact the developers and include the system details above.

{{ icon('shield', 22) }} Your Data and Privacy

  • Everything is stored only on this computer, in the data folder shown above. The app does not use the internet and sends nothing anywhere.
  • The records hold visitors' personal information, so the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) applies. See the Terms of Use below.
  • Only the person with the account can open the records. Keep the password private and log out before you leave the computer.
  • Backups hold the same personal information. Keep any USB drive with a backup in a locked place.
