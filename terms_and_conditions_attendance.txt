1. PURPOSE OF THE SYSTEM

This software helps the barangay office to:

  - manage the client queue, with priority and regular lanes;
  - keep the visitor logbook (attendance) and the records of services given.

It stores personal information on the computer where it is installed, such as clients' names, addresses and ages, the services they came for and the times they were served, and the actions of the administrator account.

The system works offline. It does not send any of this information over the internet, and the developer has no access to the barangay's records.

3. DATA PRIVACY (RA 10173)

The barangay is responsible for the personal information it records in this system. By using it, the barangay and its authorized staff agree to:

  a. Collect and record only the personal information needed to serve the client (transparency, legitimate purpose and proportionality).
  b. Tell clients why their information is collected, and respect their rights under the Data Privacy Act, such as the right to see and correct their records.
  c. Keep all client records confidential and use them only for official barangay purposes.
  d. Not disclose, copy, export or print client records for any purpose other than official barangay business, unless allowed by law or with the client's consent.
  e. Handle attendance printouts and spoiled printouts with the same care as the records, and destroy spoiled printouts securely (for example, by shredding).
  f. Keep the administrator password and the recovery key secret. The recovery key is shown only once during setup and must be stored safely, for example with the Punong Barangay.
  g. Keep regular backups of the database and store them securely. A backup contains the same personal information as the system, so a USB drive or other storage holding a backup must be kept in a locked place.
  h. Report any suspected data breach, loss or unauthorized access to the barangay's designated Data Protection Officer without delay.
  i. Keep records only as long as necessary, and dispose of them securely when they are no longer needed, for example with the system's purge of old attendance records.
