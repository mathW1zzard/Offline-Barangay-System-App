Select words and press Bold (or Ctrl+B). Put the cursor on a line and press Center (or Ctrl+E); press it again to undo. Tab indents, Shift+Tab removes it. To leave the box with the keyboard, press Esc, then Tab.

Press Enter for a new line, and Enter twice for a new paragraph (with a space before it). Press Enter once more for each extra empty line, for example after "TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN:". A field such as {Client name} is replaced when the document is printed. Words between two pairs of asterisks, like **THIS IS TO CERTIFY**, print in bold. A line starting with >> is centred, and with >>> it goes on the right. Use the buttons above so the field names are spelled correctly.
